How we check

Prices were read on 27 Nov 2026 from the store reports and named roundups cited on every deal, including dealnews, 9to5Toys, TechSpot, Android Authority, Tom’s Guide, CNN Underscored, and Yahoo Shopping. The original publication dates stay with each source.

Discounts use the listed sale and usual prices, rounded to the nearest percent. Verdicts follow the cited reports; the evidence line explains each one. Store links open a product search or the streaming service. Prices and availability can change — check at checkout.

TRUE LOW marks the source set’s lowest-price picks. STRONG DEAL marks a worthwhile reduction, including some smaller cuts on new models. ROUTINE SALE marks a modest or coupon-dependent saving. Read each verdict alongside its evidence.
